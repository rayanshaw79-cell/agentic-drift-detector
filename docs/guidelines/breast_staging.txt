BREAST CANCER STAGING — NCI/AJCC 8TH EDITION SUMMARY

SOURCE: National Cancer Institute PDQ; AJCC Cancer Staging Manual 8th Edition (2017)
Note: AJCC 8th Edition for breast cancer incorporates biomarker-based prognostic stage
groups in addition to the anatomic TNM stage. This document covers the anatomic stage.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIMARY TUMOR (T)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Tx:  Primary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0:  No evidence of primary tumor.
Tis: Carcinoma in situ.
  Tis (DCIS): Ductal carcinoma in situ.
  Tis (Paget): Paget disease of the nipple not associated with invasive carcinoma or DCIS.
T1:  Tumor ≤ 20 mm in greatest dimension.
  T1mi: Tumor ≤ 1 mm (microinvasion)
  T1a:  Tumor > 1 mm but ≤ 5 mm
  T1b:  Tumor > 5 mm but ≤ 10 mm
  T1c:  Tumor > 10 mm but ≤ 20 mm
T2:  Tumor > 20 mm but ≤ 50 mm in greatest dimension.
T3:  Tumor > 50 mm in greatest dimension.
T4:  Tumor of any size with direct extension to the chest wall and/or skin.
  T4a: Extension to chest wall (not including pectoralis muscle)
  T4b: Ulceration and/or ipsilateral satellite skin nodules and/or edema (including peau d'orange)
  T4c: Both T4a and T4b
  T4d: Inflammatory carcinoma

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
REGIONAL LYMPH NODES (N)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

N0:  No regional lymph node metastasis.
N1:  Micrometastases; or metastases in 1–3 axillary lymph nodes; and/or in internal mammary nodes.
  N1mi: Micrometastases (> 0.2 mm and/or > 200 cells, but none > 2.0 mm)
  N1a:  Metastases in 1–3 axillary lymph nodes
  N1b:  Metastases in internal mammary nodes with micrometastases or macrometastases detected by sentinel lymph node biopsy
  N1c:  N1a + N1b
N2:  Metastases in 4–9 axillary lymph nodes; or in clinically detected internal mammary nodes without axillary node metastasis.
  N2a: Metastases in 4–9 axillary lymph nodes
  N2b: Metastases in clinically detected internal mammary lymph nodes with or without microscopic confirmation, with pathologically negative axillary nodes
N3:  Metastases in ≥ 10 axillary lymph nodes; or in infraclavicular lymph nodes; or in clinically detected ipsilateral internal mammary lymph nodes in the presence of ≥ 1 positive axillary lymph nodes; or in > 3 axillary lymph nodes and in internal mammary lymph nodes; or in ipsilateral supraclavicular lymph nodes.

DISTANT METASTASIS (M):
M0: No clinical or radiographic evidence of distant metastasis.
M1: Distant metastasis detected (bone, liver, lung, brain most common sites).

ANATOMIC STAGE GROUPS (BREAST CANCER)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Stage 0:   Tis N0 M0
Stage IA:  T1 N0 M0
Stage IB:  T0-T1 N1mi M0
Stage IIA: T0-T1 N1 M0; T2 N0 M0
Stage IIB: T2 N1 M0; T3 N0 M0
Stage IIIA: T0-T2 N2 M0; T3 N1-N2 M0
Stage IIIB: T4 N0-N2 M0
Stage IIIC: Any T N3 M0
Stage IV:  Any T Any N M1

KEY BIOMARKERS AND RECEPTOR STATUS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hormone Receptor (HR) Status:
  ER-positive (ER+): ≥ 1% of tumor cells staining positive by IHC. Endocrine therapy (tamoxifen, aromatase inhibitors) is indicated.
  ER-negative (ER-): < 1% of tumor cells staining.
  PR-positive (PR+): ≥ 1% of tumor cells staining positive by IHC.

HER2 Status (ASCO/CAP 2018 Guidelines):
  HER2-positive: IHC 3+ (strong complete membrane staining > 10% of tumor cells); OR FISH with HER2/CEP17 ratio ≥ 2.0 and average HER2 ≥ 4.0 signals/cell.
  HER2-low: IHC 1+ or IHC 2+ with negative FISH.
  HER2-negative: IHC 0 or IHC 1+.
  HER2-equivocal: IHC 2+ — requires reflex FISH testing.

Breast Cancer Subtypes:
  Luminal A: ER+/PR+, HER2-, low Ki-67 (< 14%)
  Luminal B: ER+/PR+, HER2- with high Ki-67; or ER+, HER2+
  HER2-enriched: ER-, PR-, HER2+
  Triple-Negative (TNBC): ER-, PR-, HER2-

BRCA1/2 Germline Testing: Recommended for patients < 50 years, triple-negative, or with family history.
  BRCA1/2 pathogenic variant: Eligible for PARP inhibitor therapy (olaparib, talazoparib) in metastatic HER2-negative setting.

PD-L1 (CPS ≥ 10 by 22C3 assay): Pembrolizumab + chemotherapy approved for locally recurrent unresectable or metastatic TNBC.
