NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (NSCLC) — TNM DEFINITIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PRIMARY TUMOR (T):
T1: Tumor ≤ 3 cm, surrounded by lung or visceral pleura, no bronchoscopic evidence of invasion more proximal than lobar bronchus.
  T1a: Tumor ≤ 1 cm
  T1b: Tumor > 1 cm but ≤ 2 cm
  T1c: Tumor > 2 cm but ≤ 3 cm
T2: Tumor > 3 cm but ≤ 5 cm; OR tumor with any of: involves main bronchus ≥ 2 cm from carina; invades visceral pleura; associated with atelectasis or obstructive pneumonitis.
  T2a: Tumor > 3 cm but ≤ 4 cm
  T2b: Tumor > 4 cm but ≤ 5 cm
T3: Tumor > 5 cm but ≤ 7 cm; OR directly invades chest wall, phrenic nerve, or parietal pericardium; OR separate tumor nodule(s) in same lobe as primary.
T4: Tumor > 7 cm; OR tumor of any size that invades mediastinum, diaphragm, heart, great vessels, trachea, recurrent laryngeal nerve, esophagus, vertebral body, or carina; OR separate tumor nodule(s) in a different ipsilateral lobe.

REGIONAL LYMPH NODES (N):
N0: No regional lymph node metastasis.
N1: Metastasis in ipsilateral peribronchial and/or ipsilateral hilar lymph nodes, and intrapulmonary nodes; includes involvement by direct extension.
N2: Metastasis in ipsilateral mediastinal and/or subcarinal lymph nodes.
N3: Metastasis in contralateral mediastinal, contralateral hilar, ipsilateral or contralateral scalene, or supraclavicular lymph nodes.

DISTANT METASTASIS (M):
M0: No distant metastasis.
M1a: Separate tumor nodule(s) in a contralateral lobe; tumor with pleural or pericardial nodules; malignant pleural or pericardial effusion.
M1b: Single extrathoracic metastasis in a single organ.
M1c: Multiple extrathoracic metastases in one or more organs.

STAGE GROUPINGS (NSCLC)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Stage IA1: T1a N0 M0
Stage IA2: T1b N0 M0
Stage IA3: T1c N0 M0
Stage IB:  T2a N0 M0
Stage IIA: T2b N0 M0
Stage IIB: T1a-c N1 M0; T2a N1 M0; T2b N1 M0; T3 N0 M0
Stage IIIA: T1a-c N2 M0; T2a-b N2 M0; T3 N1 M0; T4 N0 M0; T4 N1 M0
Stage IIIB: T1a-c N3 M0; T2a-b N3 M0; T3 N2 M0; T4 N2 M0
Stage IIIC: T3 N3 M0; T4 N3 M0
Stage IVA: Any T, Any N, M1a or M1b
Stage IVB: Any T, Any N, M1c

SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (SCLC)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SCLC is often staged using the Veterans Administration Lung Study Group (VALSG) system:
Limited Stage: Disease confined to the ipsilateral hemithorax and regional lymph nodes that can be encompassed within a tolerable radiation port.
Extensive Stage: Disease beyond limited stage, including contralateral lung or distant metastases.

AJCC TNM staging is also applicable and follows the same T/N/M definitions as NSCLC.

KEY BIOMARKERS FOR LUNG CANCER TREATMENT SELECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EGFR mutations (exon 19 deletion, L858R exon 21, exon 20 insertion): First-line EGFR TKI therapy (osimertinib preferred).
ALK rearrangement: First-line ALK inhibitor (alectinib, brigatinib, lorlatinib).
ROS1 rearrangement: Crizotinib or entrectinib.
KRAS G12C mutation: Sotorasib or adagrasib (second-line).
BRAF V600E: Dabrafenib + trametinib.
PD-L1 TPS ≥ 50%: First-line pembrolizumab monotherapy (if no driver mutation).
PD-L1 TPS 1–49%: Pembrolizumab + platinum doublet chemotherapy.
MSI-H/dMMR: Pembrolizumab (any solid tumor, second-line).
TMB-High (≥ 10 mut/Mb): Pembrolizumab (pan-tumor indication).
