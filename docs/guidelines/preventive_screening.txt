# ICMR Preventive Screening Guidelines
━━━

## TOBACCO USERS (SMOKING, GUTKA, BEEDI, BETEL NUT)
- Annual oral cavity examination by a healthcare professional.
- Low-dose computed tomography (LDCT) for high-risk smokers (age > 50, > 20 pack-years).
- Counseling for tobacco cessation at every visit.

## ARSENIC EXPOSURE (GROUNDWATER)
- Annual dermatological examination for hyperkeratosis and skin lesions.
- Routine urinalysis for hematuria (bladder cancer risk).

## GENERAL POPULATION (AGE > 40)
- Breast: Annual clinical breast examination and biennial mammography.
- Cervical: HPV testing every 5 years or Pap smear every 3 years.
- Colorectal: Fecal immunochemical test (FIT) annually or colonoscopy every 10 years for average-risk individuals.
